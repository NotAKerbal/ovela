## People

Image: `01-people.png`

### Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished desktop screenshot of a working homelab management web app, landscape 1536x1024, full frontal edge-to-edge UI, no monitor or browser chrome or presentation framing. Use the attached image ONLY as a style reference for Mosaic Haven's warm limestone ground, muted sage/sand/slate/clay palette, fine square edges and gentle material depth. This is the same app's management area. No welcome greeting or slogans. Keep management calm, precise, readable and usable: flatter surfaces and structured rows, shallow glass accents only for primary actions, no elaborate 3D art behind data. No nested cards, decorative charts, dashboard metric boxes, or fake infrastructure stats. All text sharp, actual UI font sizes, regular dark typography.
Exactly ONE global top header: four tiny square mosaic mark and "Mosaic Haven" at left; "Home" then active "Manage" toward right; "Isaac" at far right. Below the header, ONE small horizontal management navigation with exactly "People", "Applications", "Settings"; indicate active section subtly with an underline. No sidebar, no duplicated navigation. Main body max width around 1150px, comfortable gutters, generous breathing room but no oversized hero. Square corners, warm textured paper background subtle enough to not interfere with labels. Meaningful symbols only, no decorative pills. Use fictional example users and example.com addresses.
The UI is a private household app access tool, not enterprise identity management. Admins invite people and decide whether each person can use each application. No groups, tenants, complex role builders or permission trees. Two account roles only: Member and Admin. Application access is simply on or off.
SCREEN: People index, with People active in management nav. Main section title "People" in medium-sized dark font, a restrained sage square-cornered primary button "Invite person" aligned right. Below it, a simple text input with magnifying-glass icon and placeholder "Find a person". Main content is ONE full-width table resting directly on the page, subtle horizontal dividers, no outer card. Columns "Person", "Role", "App access", "Status". Four rows:
"Isaac" and "isaac@example.com", role "Admin", app access "All applications", status "Active".
"Alex Morgan" and "alex@example.com", role "Member", app access "Photos, Files", status "Active".
"Jamie Reed" and "jamie@example.com", role "Member", app access "Photos, Media, Notes", status "Active".
"Sam Rivera" and "sam@example.com", role "Member", app access "Files", status "Invited".
Small restrained initial avatars, each row has a right chevron as clear entry to details. Status is plain text, not bright chips. Highlight the Alex row very subtly to suggest hover. After table, brief unobtrusive text "4 people". No extra actions or content. This should look like a beautiful, implementable management page with tactile restrained materials.
