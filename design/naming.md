# Name exploration

## Selected name: Ovela

Isaac selected **Ovela** after the second naming round. Pronounced oh-VELL-uh, it is a proposed construction inspired by own and vellum, fitting the personal space, paper, glass, and pigment direction. This is an intended brand story, not a historical etymology. The application and private GitHub repository are now named Ovela. Historical mockup images retain their original Mosaic Haven labels.

## Round two: coined names

Isaac's feedback: Inlay and Hearth are existing English words, and Tessera does not fit the vibe. The next exploration should go beyond ordinary nouns and classical terminology.

These are proposed constructions with intended brand stories, not verified foreign-language meanings or claims of uniqueness. None has been selected.

| Name | Pronunciation | Intended story | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mavri | MAV-ree | A compact reshaping of Mosaic Haven, preserving the idea of selected pieces becoming one home. | Its meaning relies on the project's story rather than a literal definition. |
| Avren | AV-ren | Adapted from the sound and letters of haven, aiming to sound like a place to belong. | Some readers may say AY-vren. |
| Ovela | oh-VELL-uh | Inspired by own and vellum: a personal space with a tactile material identity. | More delicate than architectural. |
| Veyra | VAY-ruh | Sound-led, inspired by weave and layer: connected parts retain their character. | The connection is invented, not etymological; spelling may need establishing. |
| Havri | HAV-ree | Inspired by haven and private: a home whose contents and access you choose. | Close to the name being replaced. |
| Mavon | MAV-on | A firmer-sounding adaptation of Mosaic Haven. | May be pronounced MAY-von, and needs the original story for meaning. |

The naming agent favors Mavri and Avren, with Ovela as the artistic alternative. Treat these as exploratory candidates. The prototype still uses Mosaic Haven.

## Round one: not selected

The name should be short, feel personal, and carry the idea of independent applications sharing one thoughtfully made home. These are creative candidates, not availability-checked names.

| Name | Meaning and fit | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inlay | Separate materials fitted into a surface to form a composition. Fits both independent apps and the glass-and-stone design. | Craftsmanship comes across more readily than home. |
| Tessera | A single mosaic piece, including square stone or glass. Pronounced TESS-er-uh. | Strong meaning, but three syllables and less familiar spelling. |
| Hearth | A fireplace, also associated with home and a gathering place. | Warm and personal, with less emphasis on assembled pieces. |
| Weft | The crosswise threads woven into fabric. Each application contributes to a connected whole. | Less familiar, and the metaphor is textile rather than glass. |
| Fold | Suggests belonging, shelter, and bringing things together. | Broad meanings, with little immediate software association. |
| Atrium | A shared architectural space with rooms opening from it. | More formal than intimate. |
| Cairn | Separate stones arranged as a recognizable marker. | Suggests wayfinding more than home. |
| Glade | A calm clearing amid surrounding complexity. | Captures the feeling rather than the connected-app idea. |
| Quoin | An architectural corner or cornerstone, pronounced like coin. | Unfamiliar spelling and possible financial associations. |
| Haven | Retains the original feeling of shelter in a shorter name. | Drops the story of pieces becoming a whole. |

## Strongest candidates

**Inlay** for the whole product. **Tessera** for the mosaic story. **Hearth** for the feeling of home.

Inlay also works in future application names: Inlay Photos, Inlay Files, and Inlay Home.
